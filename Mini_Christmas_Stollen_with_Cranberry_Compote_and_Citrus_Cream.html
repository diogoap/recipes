Kamado Preparation

The source bakes the mini Christmas stollen indirectly at 190°C for 20–25 minutes, simmers the compote for approximately ten minutes and finishes the dessert with whipped citrus crème fraîche and vanilla ice cream.

Mini Christmas Stollen

  1. Bring the butter to room temperature.
  2. Beat the butter and fine sugar together until noticeably paler and lighter.
  3. Beat the eggs together separately.
  4. Gradually add the required weight of egg while continuing to mix.
  5. Sift the plain flour and self-raising flour together.
  6. Fold the flours into the butter mixture.
  7. Mix in the cinnamon.
  8. Finely dice the cake portion of dried apricots and fold them into the batter.
  9. Lightly drain the boerenjongens. Do not squeeze them completely dry.
  10. Fold in the boerenjongens and dried cranberries.
  11. Grease the mini-tulband mould thoroughly with butter and dust it with flour.
  12. Fill each cavity to the same level that worked during your test batch.
  13. Bake indirectly at 190°C for approximately 20–25 minutes.
  14. Begin checking at 20 minutes. A skewer should not carry wet batter, although it may pick up some moisture from the fruit.
  15. Rest the cakes in the mould for approximately 5–8 minutes before turning them out.

Larger quantities produce more cakes, not larger individual cakes. Keep the cavity fill level and the per-batch cooking time unchanged, and bake in additional rounds.

Practical egg measurements

Servings Method
6 Beat 2 eggs and weigh out approximately 75 g
8 Beat 3 eggs and weigh out approximately 113 g
10 Use approximately 150 g, normally about 3 medium eggs without shells
12 Beat 4 eggs and weigh out approximately 188 g

Weighing the egg is more reliable than trying to divide the yolk and white visually.

Cranberry compote

  1. Finely dice the compote portion of the dried apricots.
  2. Put the frozen cranberries, apricots, port, sugar and approximately half the Meat Tinsel substitute into a stainless-steel saucepan.
  3. Simmer gently for approximately 10 minutes.
  4. Stir occasionally.
  5. Lightly crush only some of the cranberries, leaving others intact for texture.
  6. Taste and add more seasoning only when needed.
  7. Let the compote cool if preparing it in advance.
  8. Reheat gently before serving.
  9. Spoon the warm compote into the centre of each mini Christmas stollen.

Citrus cream

  1. Put the crème fraîche in a cold bowl.
  2. Whip it only until it becomes somewhat firmer.
  3. Add the orange and lemon zest from the table.
  4. Add the initial quantity of icing sugar.
  5. Taste and adjust until the cream is fresh, lightly sweet and clearly citrus-flavoured.
  6. Transfer it to a piping bag or spoon it onto the cakes.
  7. Dust the mini Christmas stollen with icing sugar.
  8. Serve with the warm cranberry compote and the quantity of vanilla ice cream listed in the table.
